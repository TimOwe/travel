# Sights 
## Nature/Parks
- Tiergarten, big central park in the city
- Tempelhof ! - old airport, now a huge park in the city. lots of kites in summer apparently
## Arts
- Helmut Newton Foudnation / Museum fur fotographie -- awesome photography museum for helmut newton if you like fashion photography
- Boros museum - Great gallery in an old Nazi bunker - guided tours only, bookings essential, 4yr rotating gallery cycle (I believe 2026 is a new show), great curation
## Misc
- Funkhaus Berlin, looks beautiful, hosts Jazz i believe
- East side gallery (?) is a preserved section of the berlin wall near Berghain
