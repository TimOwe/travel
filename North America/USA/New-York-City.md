# General info
- For public transport, you can use your credit card via apple/google pay, and enable express transit for faster tap on
- Ubers/Taxi/Lyft are really expensive
- CitiBike is awesome but the per-ride cost is prohibitive without the monthly membership, you'll get good value out of it if you're there in summer

# Eating Out

## Bagels
- Everyone has their own hot take. a fresh bagel trumps all;
- Russ & Daughters - classic oldschool Jewish deli - you can go to the original location on Houston and get a sandwich takeaway, or go to their cafe location around the corner on Orchard st (pricey though)
- Kossar's nearby is also a good option for a fresh out of the oven bagel
## Pizza
- Everyone has their own hot take;
- Scarrs, Mama's Too, Williamsburg pizza all do great slices

## Queens
- [New world mall, Flushing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hgDjyZSZyFhBUet59) - great food court, flushing is the chinatown outside of chinatown, generally better food and cheaper than manhattan chinatown but can be a bit of a trek
- Nurlan Uyghur Restaurant
- Joe’s Steam Rice Roll (Locations also in Manhattan)
- White Bear

## Brooklyn
- Roberta's - great pizza
- Win Son Bakery (Also a location in east village manhattan) - Taiwanese with modern twist. good, but far from traditional

- Scarr's Pizza

## Clubs
Parties to check if they're on:
Zero Chill, Faggots are women, Spaghetti Strap, Merge, Honey Trap, Groovy Groovy, Club Night Club


- Signal - Smaller club with an amazing sound system and great curation. Closer to civilisation than some of the deeper ridgewood-adjacent clubs.. hosts queer parties often; check their instagram
- Gabriela - Smaller club, usually all night long DJ sets
- Nowadays - Best club the city, totally unbiased opinion - aim for morning arrival if there's a nonstop party on (8am-miday is beautiful). On summers, mister sunday in the afternoon is really fun too
- Basement - IMO this is berlin cosplay and it leans corny, but they book good artists
- H0L0 - Cute smaller club with great sound deep in bushwick, crowd varies. good after late late nights
- Public Records - Crowd can be absolutely demonic but great bookings. Has a bar and a dancefloor ("the soundroom")


### Gay Bars
- Animal - Good sound system, good drinks, friendly crowd, bears every thursday night (go from 7ish)
- Eagle NYC - Classicly a leather bar that only opens late but now they charge a cover on weekends and there's a huge dance floor. Sunday arvo is good
- Rockbar - hit or miss, but has some busy nights, check their IG; i'd stick to weekends
- Ty's - old guy dive bar. Usually some bears. Busy right after work or on weekends
- Gym bar - Gay "sports bar" that has a 2-for-1 happy hour after work which is almost always busy
- Happy Fun Hideaway - Gay bar in bushwick, 
- Midtown + Chelsea are full of a horde of almost identical non-descript gay bars

# Shopping
## Misc 
- 50 Norman in williamsburg/greenpoint is very cute for japanese homewares/clothing/cookware, but pricey
- FAO Schwarz is technically a toy shop but it's the one from Home alone 2 (though it's since moved), it's fun to see, and if there's a line it moves quickly. Expect chaos
- B&H is a legendary electronics store in midtown with a huge amount of stock of everything you can imagine techwise, and an elaborate conveyer belt/elevator system carting stock around the store. its very cool

## Clothing
- L Train Vintage - Several locations, CHEAP vintage compared to aus
- Hatchet Outdoor Supply Co. - Gorpcore in brooklyn
- Ven. Space - Higher end menswear boutique w great buyers
- Dover Street Market for avant garde 
- Komune (higher end)
- Fugazi (streetwear)
- Metropolis Vintage (tons of polo, good t shirt selection)
- Second Street - higher end secondhand stuff, always worth popping in if you're walking past one
- Luke's - Higher end second hand, well curated, well priced
- HAPPY99 - NYC brand with a small but very charming store in chinatown
- Blue in Green - Japanese brands like Kapital in Soho
- Telfar - the most new york shopping experience ever


## Records
- Not records, but Synth shop "Control" in brooklyn has great stock
- The Thing - Greenpoint record store with literally thousands of records. Expect to dig. I think Baba Stiltz works(/ed) here
- A1 Records - Ron Morelli used to work here. The best in the city for dance music


## Bookshops
- Hive Mind Books & Coffee Shop - Great queer bookshop in Bushwick
- McNally Jackson - I think they have a few locations, always good curation
- The Strand
- Casa Magazines 

# Sights 
## Nature/Parks
- A walk through central park is always nice,
- Prospect Park is also quite beautiful and the botanical gardens is great for cherry blossoms if they're in season
- The Highline is a great walk and ends at Hudson Yards if you take it northbound. 
## Arts
- MoMa, the Met, Guggenheim, are all good, and close-ish to eachother,
-- Met Cloisters is awesome if you want to go uptown
- The Frick has a great classical collection but imo entry is too expensive
- Dia Beacon is well worth a day trip if you have the time
- Whitney is alright but again, too expensive imo
- International Center of Photography Museum for photography
- Brooklyn Museum often has great exhhibits
- The Gagosian museums near Chelsea Markets are all free entry and walking around here and popping into all the smaller commercial galleries is a good afternoon
## Misc
- Keith Haring's Bathroom Mural - worth popping in to see if you're nearby; you can ask the staff to direct you
